# Study Wall Lighthouse

This is a little animation of a brass lighthouse lens on a dim study wall. A warm beam sweeps slowly around the wall, and the glass panels of the lens turn with it.

Now at step 3 of 5 I have added soft dust motes. They drift upward in the dark, barely visible, and sparkle when the beam passes over them. Everything is drawn with canvas, no pictures. Next I want a click to change the beam's speed, and a gentle lamp hum.

To look at it, just open the page. It resizes to fit your screen, phone included.
