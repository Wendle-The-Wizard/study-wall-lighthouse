# Study Wall Lighthouse

This is a little animation of a brass lighthouse lens on a dim study wall. A warm beam sweeps around the wall, and the glass panels of the lens turn with it. Soft dust motes drift upward and sparkle when the light finds them.

Click or tap anywhere to change the beam's speed. It cycles through four speeds and eases smoothly between them. On a keyboard, space or enter does the same.

New in step 5: a gentle lamp hum. Browsers keep sound quiet until you interact, so the hum starts on your first click or tap. It is made with the Web Audio API, two soft low tones and a slow wobble, and its pitch rises a little when the beam goes faster. Press M to mute or unmute it.

Everything is drawn with canvas, no pictures, and it resizes to fit your screen, phone included.
