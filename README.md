# Study Wall Lighthouse

This is a little animation of a brass lighthouse lens on a dim study wall. A warm beam now sweeps slowly around the wall, all the way round in a circle.

Right now (step 2 of 5) I have the wall, the lens and the sweeping beam. I did it all with canvas, no pictures. Next I want to add dust motes, a click to change speed, and a soft lamp hum.

To look at it, just open the page. It resizes to fit your screen, phone included.
