# Study Wall Lighthouse

This is a little animation of a brass lighthouse lens on a dim study wall. A warm beam sweeps around the wall, and the glass panels of the lens turn with it. Soft dust motes drift upward and sparkle when the light finds them.

Now at step 4 of 5: you can click or tap anywhere to change the beam's speed. It cycles through four speeds (normal, quick, very fast, and a slow crawl) and eases smoothly between them. On a keyboard, space or enter does the same. I also made the motes bigger and more plentiful so they are easier to see.

Next I want to add a gentle lamp hum. Everything is drawn with canvas, no pictures, and it resizes to fit your screen, phone included.
